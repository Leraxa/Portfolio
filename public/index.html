Explore the project↓
01 / THE VISION

Make every pull count.

A combat vision built around positioning, controlling groups of enemies and combining area skills. Active play and meaningful progression are at the heart of the design.

02 / THE FOUNDATION

A world the server owns.

A dedicated server owns movement and combat. Shared C# gameplay rules are separated from Unity, with persistent character, inventory and loot systems underneath.

03 / THE CRAFT

Code meets atmosphere.

Alongside the gameplay prototype, dedicated art scenes explore stylized rendering, cave lighting and waterfalls in Unity's Universal Render Pipeline.
